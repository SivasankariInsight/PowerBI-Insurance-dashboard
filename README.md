## Business Problem Statement

Insurance organizations handle large volumes of policies, claims, premium collections, and customer data. Without a centralized reporting system, it becomes difficult to monitor performance, track claims, and identify business opportunities.

This dashboard provides a **single interactive view** of the insurance business, enabling faster and data-driven decision-making.

### Key Business Questions

* Which insurance products generate maximum premium revenue?
* What is the claim liability against collected premium?
* How many policies are active vs inactive?
* Which customer age group contributes higher claim costs?
* What is the claim status (Pending / Settled / Rejected)?
* Which policy categories need strategic focus?

## Project Objective

Design and develop an end-to-end Power BI dashboard to transform raw insurance data into actionable business insights for stakeholders.

## Project Workflow

### Data Preparation

* Imported insurance dataset from structured source files
* Cleaned and transformed data using Power Query
* Removed duplicates and handled missing values
* Standardized formats and corrected data types
* Validated data quality using profiling tools

### 🔗 Data Modeling

* Created relationships between customer, policy, and claims tables
* Built an optimized data model for reporting

### DAX Measures Created

* Total Premium Amount
* Total Coverage Amount
* Total Claim Amount
* Active Policies Count
* Inactive Policies Count
* Claim Status Counts
* Premium by Policy Type
* Claim Amount by Age Group

### Dashboard Development

* Added slicers for Policy Number, Claim Number, Customer ID
* Designed KPI cards for Premium, Coverage, and Claims
* Built visuals for trends, claims status, age analysis, and policy performance
* Applied professional formatting and interactive navigation

## Executive Summary

The dashboard highlights strong premium performance across key policy categories while identifying inefficiencies in claims processing.

Travel and Health insurance contribute the highest revenue, while inactive customers present opportunities for retention strategies.

---

## Key Business Insights

### Revenue Insights

* Travel insurance generates the highest premium revenue
* Health and Auto policies show strong performance
* Home and Life policies present growth opportunities

### claims Insights

* High number of rejected claims requires root-cause analysis
* Pending claims may impact customer satisfaction
* Settled claims indicate operational efficiency

### Customer Insights

* Adult customers contribute the highest claim amount
* Young adults represent a lower-risk segment

### Portfolio Insights

* Active policies exceed inactive policies
* Inactive customers can be targeted for retention

---

## Strategic Recommendations

* Focus on high-performing products like Travel and Health
* Reduce pending claims through automation
* Analyze rejection reasons and improve processes
* Launch retention campaigns for inactive customers
* Target low-risk segments with personalized offers
* Monitor claim-to-premium ratio for profitability
